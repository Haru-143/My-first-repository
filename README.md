# My Favorite Anime
## Seishun Buta Yarou wa Bunny Girl Senpai no Yume wo Minai

One of my favorite Anime series is *Seishun Buta Yarou wa Bunny Girl Senpai no Yume wo Minai*. I watched this series when the world was still in the pandemic and this anime quickly made me fall in love with it. This anime at first is all about the main character named *Sakuta* with a disease called *Adolescence Syndrome*, this syndrome caused many anomolies to happen around him and the first anomoly he encountered was *Mai Sakurajima* in the library wearing a Bunny girl suit with no one in the library but him being able to see her. This kickstarts the relationship between them and with him trying to cure her Anomoly as he stays besides her because if he fell asleep he won't be able to see her again and *Mai* won't be seen, but *Sakuta* has an exam coming up and he tried everthing to stay awake so he won't forget *Mai" but then he had an idea while in an exam so everyone around her won't forget her, *Sakuta* ran outside of the school to the court and there he confessed his love to *Mai* screaming everything he says for everyone to hear and this makes everyone not forget her anymore and this starts the relationship of *Sakuta* and *Mai*. After this encounter *Sakuta* continued to discover anomoly for the rest of the series and this is the main premise. This is my favorite anime because I am a suck for Romance Series/Movies.
